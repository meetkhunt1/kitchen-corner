The Story Behind The Flavor
Our Founder’s Story

The First Batch

Ashray created the very first ginger garlic paste in his home kitchen, testing small batches, tweaking recipes, and learning what real consistency means.

2019

The Street-to-Store Hustle

He walked store to store across Rajkot, pitching samples and building trust the hard way. Every yes came from effort, not branding.

2020

Forty Stores, One Foundation

Retailers began stocking the product regularly. Not because of packaging, but because the flavour stayed reliable every single time.

2021

Building From Scratch

From sourcing to manufacturing, every step was learned hands-on. No shortcuts, no outsourcing, only trial, error, and steady progress.

2022–24

Kitchen Corner Is Born

The dream turned into a brand. Clean ingredients, consistent taste, and a commitment to real flavour became the identity people trust today.

2025
